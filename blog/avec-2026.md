# Presenting at AVEC 2026 in Japan

*September 7–11, 2026 · Tsukuba, Japan*

I presented our paper, [Racing a Wheeled Quadruped: Active Load Transfer Mitigation via Model Predictive Control]({{ '/wheeled-quadruped/' | relative_url }}), at the 17th International Symposium on Advanced Vehicle Control (AVEC) in Tsukuba, Japan.

This was my first time attending (and presenting) at a conference and I learned so much. It felt extremely rewarding to meet other researchers from across the world and make strong connections for the future. I got lots of engaging questions and discussion after my presentation which made me excited for my future work regarding this project. While watching other talks, the notable topics that stood out to me were on high-fidelity simulation and transfer to real world. It seemed like many of the presentations lacked real experimentation and only had simulated validation while other works highlighted the development of simulators. This suggested to me that a major challenge and bottleneck in modern autonomous vehicle research is how to safely and effectively demonstrate theoretical advancements in realistic situations, where the conditions are not perfectly controlled or predicted.
