input-output linearization

example: design u to solve tracking problem (\(x_2 \rightarrow x_2^d\))
\(\dot x_1=a\sin x_2\)
\(\dot x_2=-x_1^2+u\)
define output \(y=x_2(y \rightarrow y^d=x_2^d)\)
\(u=x_1^2+v\)
\(\implies \dot x_1=a\sin x_2;\dot x_2=v\)
\(v=-k(y=y^d)\)
\(y=x_2\)

linearize input-output map \(\bf{\dot y = v}\)
input-output linearized system achieved by \(u=-x_1^2+v\)
\(x_1\) is unobservable from output y
\(x_1\) should be stable OR at least bounded (internal stability)

generalize to SISO system:
\(\dot x = f(x)+g(x)u,x\in \mathbb{R}^n,u\in\mathbb{R}\)
\(y=h(x),y\in\mathbb{R}\)
f,g,h are sufficiently smooth

output dynamics:
\(\dot y = \frac{\partial h}{\partial x}\dot x= \frac{\partial h}{\partial x}[f(x)+g(x)u]=L_fh(x)+L_gh(x)u\)
suppose \(L_gh(x)\not = 0\)
\(u=L_gh(x)^{-1}\{L_fh(x)+v\}\implies \dot y =v\)
if \(L_g h(x) = 0 \implies \dot y = L_fh(x)\)
\(\ddot y = \frac{\partial L_f h}{\partial x}[f(x)+g(x)u]=L_f(L_fh(x))+L_g(L_fh(x))u\)
\(=L_f^2h(x)+L_gL_fh(x) u\)

suppose \(L_gL_fh(x)\not = 0,u=L_gL_fh(x)^{-1}\{-L_f^2h(x)+v\}\)
\(\implies \ddot y = v\)

if \(L_gL_fh(x)=0,\ddot y = L_f^2h(x)\)
\(\dot {\ddot y}=L_f^3h(x)+L)gL_f^2h(x)u\) \(\implies \dot{\ddot y} = v\)
\(\vdots\)
\(y^r=L_f^rh(x)+L_gL_f^{r-1}h(x)u\)
\(L_gL_f^{h-1}h(x)\not = 0 \implies u = L_gL_f^{r-1}h(x)^{-1}\{-L_f^rh(x)+v\}\implies y^r=v\)
r is the relative degree of y
