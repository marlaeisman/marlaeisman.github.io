exam review
manifolds: \(M \in \mathbb{R}^n\) is a m-dim smooth manifold of \(\mathbb{R}^n\) if \(\forall p \in M, \exists r>0, F:B_r(P)\rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{n-m}\) s.t.
a) \(M \cap B_r(P)=\{x \in B_r(P)|F(x)=0\}\)
b) F smooth
c) F local diff, i.e. \(\forall \bar x \in M \cap B_r(P), \frac{\partial F}{\partial x}(\bar x)\) has rank n-m

region of attraction:
\(R_a(x_e=\{x_0\in\mathbb{R}^n|x(t,x_0\rightarrow x_e \text{ as } t \rightarrow \infty\}\)
\(\lim_{t\rightarrow \infty}x(t,x_0)=x_e\)

\(\dot V<0\) in D then \(R_A(x_e)=D\) is FALSE

sum of squares
\(\dot x = f(x)+g(x)u,\quad f(x_e)=0\)
\(x_e\) is AS

find CLF for \(\Sigma_L \quad V(x)=x^\top P x\)
solve \(A^\top P + PA = -Q\)
as valid CLF globally

backstepping

\(\dot x = f_0(x)+g_0(x)\zeta_1 \quad \dot \zeta_1=f_1(x,\zeta_1)+g_1(x,\zeta_1)u\)
stabilize by designing \(\zeta_1=\alpha(x)\) and \(V(x)\) is lyapunov form
design \(u=\alpha_1(x_1,\zeta_1)\) s.t. \(\zeta_1\rightarrow \alpha(x)\)
\(V_1(x,\zeta_1)=V(x)+1/2(\zeta_1-\alpha(x))^2\)

sliding mode

\(\dot x_1 = x_2 \quad \dot x_2 = h(x)+g(x)u\)
define \(s=ax_1+x_2\) on \(s\implies s \equiv 0 \implies x_2=-ax_1 \implies \dot x_1=-ax_1\)
\(x_1,x_2\rightarrow 0\)
dynamics off of s: \(\dot s = a \dot x_1 + \dot x_2 = ax_2 + [h(x)+g(x)u]\)
\(V(s)=1/2s^2\quad \dot V(s) = s \dot s\)
\(|\frac{ax_2+h(x)}{g(x)}|\le\sigma(x)\)
substitute into \(\dot V\rightarrow\) \(\dot V \le \sigma(x)g(x)|s|+g(x)su\)
then we need to design u
choose \(u=-\beta(x)(\text{sign} s),\quad \beta(x) \ge \sigma(x) +\beta_0\)
\(\dot V(s) \le -g(x)|s|\beta_0\le -g_0\beta_0|s|\)
\(s\rightarrow 0\) in finite time on \(s=0,x\rightarrow 0,t\rightarrow \infty\)
