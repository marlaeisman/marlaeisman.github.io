corollary: suppose \(\dot x = f(t,x)\) is p.w. continuous in t and \(\exists r>0,T>0,L<\infty\) s.t. \(||\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(t,x)||\le L,\forall x \in B_r(x_0),t\in[0,T]\) then, \(\exists \delta >0\) s.t. \(\dot x = f(t,x),x(t_0)=x_0\) has exactly one solution

  • slightly stronger than just Lipschitz

theorem (3.2 khalil): global existence and uniqueness:

  • suppose \(f(t,x)\) is p.w. continuous in t & \(\forall T \in [t_0,\infty),\exists L_T<\infty\) s.t. \(||f(t,x)-f(t,y)||\le L_T ||x-y|| \forall x,y \in R^n, \forall t \in [t_0,T]\)
  • this tells us then \(\forall T \in [t_0,\infty),\dot x(t,x),x(t_0)=x_0\) has exactly one solution on \([t_0,T]\).
  • global Lipschitz \(\implies\) global uniqueness
  • we can then evaluate finite escape time

examples:

  1. \(\dot x(t) = Ax(t)+Bu(t),x(t_0)=x_0 \in R^n\)
    • \(f(t,x)=Ax(t)+Bu(t)\)
    • f is p.w. cont. in t \(\iff\) u(t) is p.w. cont. in t
    • \(||f(t,x)-f(t,y)||=||Ax-Ay||=||A(x-y)||\le||A||_{\bar A}||x-y||\)
      • \(||A||_{\bar A}\) must be finite for Lipschitz
    • \(\implies\) global existence + uniqueness
  2. \(\dot x(t)=1+x(t)^2,x(0)=0\)
    • know \(x(t)=\tan (t)\)
    • finite escape time at \(t=\pi / 2\)
    • \(f(x)=1+x^2\)
    • \(||\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}||=||2x||\)
    • \(||\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}||\le 2\) when \(-1 \le x \le 1 \implies\) f locally Lipschitz \(\implies\) local existence + uniqueness

gronwall-bellman inequality:

  • intuition: suppose \(\dot y = \mu (t) y(t), y(a)=\lambda \iff y(t)=\lambda + \int^t_a \mu(\tau)y(\tau)d \tau\)
  • \(\iff\) \(y(t) = \lambda \exp[\int^t_a \mu(\tau)d \tau]\)
  • then: suppose \(\lambda \in R\) is a ocnstant & \(\mu:[a,b]\rightarrow R\) is continuous non-negative
    • under these conditions, function \(y:[a,b]\rightarrow R\) satisfies implicit inequality \(y(t) \le \lambda + \int^t_a \mu(\tau)y(\tau)d \tau\) for all \(a \le t \le b\)
  • then: \(y(t) \le \lambda + \int^t_a \mu(\tau)d \tau\) for all \(t \in [a,b]\)

continuous dependance on initial conditions:

  • \(\dot x = f(t,x)\), \(x(t_0)=x_0 \in R^n\)
  • define \(\Psi:R^n\rightarrow C_n[t_0,T]\)
    • \(\Psi(x_0)(t):=x(t,t_0,x_0)\)
  • \(\Psi\) is cont. at \(x_0\) if \(\forall \varepsilon > 0, \exists \delta > 0\)
    • s.t. \(||x_0-x_0||<\delta \implies ||\Psi(x_0)-\Psi(z_0)||_\infty < \varepsilon\)
    • \(x_0 \in B_\delta (x_0) \implies \Psi(Z_0) \in B_\varepsilon (\Psi (x_0))\)

theorem: continuous dependence on initial conditions

  • considers \(\dot x = f(t,x),x\in R^n,f(t,x)\) satisfies the conditions of the global existence + uniqueness theorem
  • fix \(T \in [t_0,\infty]\) & suppose \(x()\&z()\) are 2 solutions satisfying \(\dot x = f(t,x),x(t_0)=x_0\) and \(\dot z = f(t,z),x(t_0)=z_0\) .
    • under these conditions, \(\forall \varepsilon > 0, \exists \delta > 0\)\( s.t. \)||x_0-z_0||<\delta \implies \sup_{t_0 \le t \le T}|x(t)-z(t)||<\varepsilon$.

proof: \(x(t)=x_0+\int_{t_0}^tf(\tau,x(\tau))d\tau, z(t)=z_0+\int_{t_0}^tf(\tau,z(\tau))d\tau, t_0 \le t \le T\)

  • \(x(t)-z(t)=x_0-z_0+\int_{t_0}^tf(\tau,x(\tau))-f(\tau,z(\tau))d\tau\)
  • \(||x(t)-z(t)||\le ||x_0-z_0||+\int_{t_0}^tf(\tau,x(\tau))-f(\tau,z(\tau))d\tau\)
  • because f is globally Lipschitz, \(\le||x_0-z_0||+\int_{t_0}^tL_T||x(\tau)-z(\tau)||d\tau\)
  • \(y(t)=||x(t)-z(t)||,\lambda=||x_0-z_0||,\mu(t)=L_T\)
  • \(\implies ||x(t)-z(t)|| \le ||x_0-z_0||\exp(L_T(t-t_0))\)
  • \(\sup_{t_0 \le t \le T}||x(t)-z(t)||_\infty \le ||x_0-z_0|| \exp(L_T(T-t_0))\)
  • given \(\varepsilon < 0,\) choose \(\delta = \frac{\varepsilon}{\exp(L_T(T-t_0))}\)
