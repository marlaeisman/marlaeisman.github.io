# Volunteer Work

### Haven Hospice
- I volunteer weekly with Haven Hospice in the No One Dies Alone program and have accrued **30+ hours** in the last year.
- Activities include one-on-one companionship, music therapy, and short visits to provide comfort to patients and families.
- Learn more [here](https://havenhospice.com/).

### UF Society of Women Engineers
- Volunteer at **2+ events per semester**, leading hands-on STEM activities for K-8 students (Introduce A Girl To Engineering Day, Engineering Fair).
- Designed interactive lessons (e.g., Florida Aquifer water-filter activity) to teach engineering concepts with low-cost materials.
