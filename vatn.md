# AUV Autonomy: Model-Based Control for Underwater Vehicles (Vatn Systems, 2025)

**Summary:**
As an Autonomy Engineering Intern (Mar–Aug 2025) I developed model-based controllers and integration stacks for AUVs and contributed to Vatn’s first multi-AUV swarm demonstration.

**Key results:**
- Designed a model-based LQR controller coupling six-degree-of-freedom vehicle dynamics to actuated fins, rudder, and thruster; validated performance in Python/C simulation and through real-world field tests.
- Helped deliver Vatn’s first multi-AUV swarm demonstration integrating acoustic communication, Doppler Velocity Log (DVL) feedback, and machine learning for coordinated stability in challenging ocean conditions.

**Technical details:**
- Implementation: Python/C simulation, ROS 2 integration, and on-board navigation stacks with custom inertial navigation components.
